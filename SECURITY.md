# Security Policy

## Supported surface

Security reports are accepted for the current `main` branch and the production
Worker at `https://mt5-online-index.meme-top-5-index.workers.dev`.

## Reporting

Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting for this repository. Do not open a
public issue containing an active exploit, secret, private key, personal data,
or custody detail.

Include:

- affected endpoint, commit, or transaction
- reproduction steps
- expected impact
- whether funds, mint authority, reserve data, or index integrity may be at
  risk
- a safe contact method

## Response

The project will acknowledge a complete report, classify severity, preserve
evidence, and follow `docs/security/INCIDENT_RESPONSE.md`. Creation,
redemption, and Mainnet operations remain disabled during any unresolved high
or critical issue.

No safe-harbor or reward promise is made until counsel approves a formal bug
bounty policy.
